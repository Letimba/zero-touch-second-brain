integration into another ecosystem.

## Differentiation to lead with

1. **Zero-touch**: clipboard → session → lessons → retrieval → hygiene without
   manual filing.
2. **Android/Termux native**: almost nobody serves this platform well.
3. **Benchmarked collaboration**: 100/100 routing suite, public and repeatable.
4. **Honest safety**: explicit statement that redaction is pattern-based.
